# Code of Conduct

## The short version

Be decent. Assume the other person is trying to help. Criticise code, not
people.

## What is expected

- Keep discussion on the technical substance.
- Accept that maintainers may decline a change without it being a judgement
  of you.
- Remember that most people here are doing this in their spare time, in a
  second or third language.

## What is not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks
- Sexualised language or imagery
- Publishing other people's private information
- Deliberately derailing threads or wasting reviewers' time

## Scope

This applies to issues, pull requests, discussions, commit messages, and any
space where you are representing the project.

## Enforcement

Report problems to **@TechnicalSerena** on Telegram. Reports are handled
privately. Maintainers may edit or delete comments, close threads, or block
accounts, in proportion to what happened and whether it continues.
